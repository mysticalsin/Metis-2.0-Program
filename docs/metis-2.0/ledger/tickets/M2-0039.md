## Summary

With backgroundThrottling:false the renderer's visibilityState never turns hidden, so the Bar orb's RAF loop (and the three.js obsidian orb) paints at full rate all day, and the main process polls the cursor at about 42 Hz during Hide/Island rest. Gate animation on a main-to-renderer presenter signal and window blur, and make cursor polling adaptive, keeping reveal responsive.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Orb RAF loops pause or drop to at most 8 fps when the window has been blurred for N seconds or the presenter is parked; the signal comes from main, not visibilityState; reduced motion honoured
- [ ] Cursor-watch runs at 24 ms only near the top edge or during transitions and at least 250 ms otherwise; reveal latency p95 stays at or under 150 ms
- [ ] Before/after census: parked idle at or under 1% of one core over 5 min (MASTER section 7), or the residual documented in ADR-018
- [ ] Frame-time p95 at or under 16.7 ms during reveal and active states, measured on the packaged app
- [ ] Logic lives in a new module; src/main/index.ts changes are call-site swaps only and land through the merge queue in the declared order (M2-0188)
