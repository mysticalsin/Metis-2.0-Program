## Summary

With the journal guaranteeing acknowledged revisions, a wedged renderer can be recovered automatically. Renderer generations plus hello/heartbeat/ping detect a wedge; recovery uses forcefullyCrashRenderer then reload with a budget and a SAFE_MODE dialog; ADR-004 supersedes the old 'no reload' pin. A blocked main thread is out of scope for any in-process controller.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Generations increment on every load; renderer sends hello after app init and heartbeat every 2 s; main pings with a 1 s pong deadline; stale-generation acks ignored
- [ ] SUSPECT after 6 s without heartbeat; escalate after 10 s (30 s during capture); success = hello(new gen) + journal restored + pong
- [ ] 2 recoveries per 10 min, then SAFE_MODE parented native dialog; explicit capture.discontinuity; no replayed actions
- [ ] RC-1..6 fault injection (busy loop, kill, failed preload) pass on both OS lanes; flag reveal.autoRecover
