## Summary

The window.confirm theory of the freeze is refuted (Electron 43 parents the sheet), but nine native confirm/alert calls in a frameless always-on-top overlay are poor UX and block the renderer while open. Replace them with one in-DOM guard whose Cancel preserves the recap edit, and ban the APIs with an AST check.
