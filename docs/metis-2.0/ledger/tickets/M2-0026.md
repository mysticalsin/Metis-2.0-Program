## Summary

Sidecars are torn down by two duplicated hand-written lists (will-quit and the Cmd+Ctrl+Esc hard exit), the onFatal 'Relaunch' path calls app.relaunch(); app.exit(0) with no teardown, and the ffmpeg import decoder and watch-frontmost helper are in neither list. Replace both lists with one ordered stopAll() used by every exit path. Do not add a SIGTERM handler or 'detached' flags: Electron already maps SIGTERM to app.quit and the verifier's experiment showed both are irrelevant to the orphan.
